## Mission Statement

Content Forge exists to transform scattered information from trusted sources into high-quality, platform-optimized content through an AI-powered editorial pipeline — enabling creators, marketers, and businesses to publish consistently across multiple platforms without manual repetition.

---

## Core Values

1. **Automation** — Automate the entire pipeline from collection to publication, eliminating repetitive manual work.
2. **Quality Assurance** — Multi-stage AI Agent verification combined with human review delivers a hybrid QA process that ensures trustworthy output.
3. **Multi-Platform Native** — One source produces optimized content for Blog, LinkedIn, Instagram, X, and more — each formatted for its native audience and constraints.
4. **Transparency** — Every pipeline stage records its intermediate output, so users can inspect, understand, and intervene at any point.
5. **Progressive Automation** — Start with human review for every piece; as trust builds through consistent quality, graduate to auto-approval and hands-free publishing.

---

## North Star Metric

> **Monthly active users who complete at least one full pipeline run (source-to-publish) per week.**

**Current baseline:** 0 (pre-launch)
**Target (12 months):** 500 weekly-active pipeline users

---

## Target Users

| Persona | Description | Primary Need |
|---------|-------------|--------------|
| **Personal Branding Creator** | Professionals with domain expertise who want consistent multi-platform presence | Curate + AI-process content efficiently, publish to 3+ platforms from one workflow |
| **Small Marketing Team / Agency** | Teams managing multiple client channels with limited headcount | Systematized content production pipeline with quality controls and scheduling |
| **SaaS Subscriber** | General users who subscribe to build their own content pipelines | Easy-to-use, self-service content automation with flexible pipeline configuration |

---

## Differentiators

| # | Differentiator | Why It Matters |
|---|---------------|----------------|
| 1 | **Editorial Room Metaphor** | AI Agents with distinct roles (Analyst, Writer, Editor, Designer, etc.) mirror a real newsroom, producing more structured and higher-quality output than single-prompt generators |
| 2 | **Pipeline Visibility** | Full transparency into every stage's input/output — unlike black-box content tools |
| 3 | **Platform-Native Preview** | See exactly how content will appear on LinkedIn, X, Instagram, and Blog before publishing |
| 4 | **Trust-Based Automation** | Progressive auto-approval system that earns trust through repeated quality verification |
| 5 | **Source-to-Publish E2E** | The only all-in-one solution covering collection, processing, review, and publishing — competitors address only fragments of the workflow |

## Success Criteria

By **2026-09-19** (6 months from initial scaffolding), we will consider the project successful when:

- [ ] **MRR > $10K** — Monthly recurring revenue from paid subscriptions
- [ ] **500+ weekly-active pipeline users** — Users completing at least 1 pipeline run/week
- [ ] **5% free-to-paid conversion rate** — From Free tier to Creator/Pro/Team
- [ ] **< 5% monthly churn** — Retention of paying subscribers
- [ ] **Auto-approval adoption > 30%** — Paying users enabling auto-approval on at least one pipeline
- [ ] **3+ platforms per user average** — Users publishing to multiple platforms from a single pipeline
